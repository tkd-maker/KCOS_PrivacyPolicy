KCOS Capture — Support

KCOS Capture is Kingsbury Companies’ field app for uploading jobsite photos to KCOS. It is for Kingsbury employees and approved partners; a Kingsbury (Microsoft) account is required to sign in.

Getting help

For help with the app — sign-in problems, missing projects, or upload issues — contact:

  • Email: help@kingsburyco.com

Common questions

  • I can’t sign in. You must use your Kingsbury Microsoft account. If you don’t have one or aren’t approved yet, contact us at the email above.
  • I don’t see my project. You only see projects you’ve been given access to. Contact us to be added to a project.
  • My photos say “waiting to upload.” The app queues photos when you’re offline and uploads them automatically once you’re back on a connection.
