KCOS Capture — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8.4.2026

KCOS Capture (“the app”) is an internal application provided by Kingsbury Companies (“Kingsbury,” “we,” “us”) for our employees and approved partners to photograph jobsites and upload those photos to Kingsbury’s project system (KCOS). This policy also covers operational notifications that Kingsbury sends through KCOS, including email and SMS text messages such as work-schedule releases. It explains what the app and these services collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Photos you choose to upload. When you capture or select a photo and tap upload, the app sends that image, its file name, and its capture date/time to KCOS so it can be filed under the correct project.
  • Account identity. You sign in with your Microsoft (Entra ID) account. We receive your name, email/username, and a user identifier to authenticate you and to record who uploaded each photo.
  • Mobile phone number. If you receive text notifications, we use the mobile number in your Kingsbury employee record to send operational messages and to record your opt-in and opt-out status.
  • Basic operational data. Limited technical information needed to run the app reliably (for example, error and connectivity status for the upload queue, and message delivery status for text notifications).

How we use it

We use this information solely to (1) sign you in, (2) show you the projects you’re authorized to access, (3) attach the photos you upload to the correct project in KCOS, and (4) send you operational notifications you have opted in to receive. We do not sell your data, share it with third parties for advertising, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Device permissions

The app requests access to your camera and photo library only to let you take or select the photos you choose to upload. It does not access other content, and it does not upload anything you don’t explicitly select and send.

Text message (SMS) program

Kingsbury Companies sends SMS text messages to employees for operational purposes, including work-schedule releases and job-related updates. Participation is voluntary.

  • Consent. You will only receive these messages if you have provided your mobile number and opted in to receive texts from Kingsbury Companies. The first time we contact you, you will be asked to reply YES to confirm; we will not send further messages unless you confirm.
  • Message frequency. Frequency varies with schedule and operational activity (typically a few messages per week).
  • Costs. Message and data rates may apply, according to the terms of your mobile carrier and plan. Kingsbury Companies is not responsible for these charges.
  • Opting out. You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, we will send a one-time confirmation and you will no longer receive texts from us. To rejoin, reply START.
  • Help. Reply HELP, or contact us at [OFFICE PHONE] / help@kingsburyco.com.
  • Delivery. Message delivery is subject to your mobile carrier; carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Your privacy. We use a third-party messaging provider (Twilio) solely to deliver these messages on our behalf. We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in/consent information with any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. Mobile information is used only to operate the text-messaging program described here.

Storage and retention

Uploaded photos and related records are stored within Kingsbury’s KCOS system and its service providers, and are retained according to Kingsbury’s business record-keeping practices.

Access

Access to the app and to uploaded photos is limited to authorized Kingsbury personnel and approved partners.

Your choices

You control which photos you upload, and whether you receive text notifications (reply STOP at any time to opt out). To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact us at the address below.
