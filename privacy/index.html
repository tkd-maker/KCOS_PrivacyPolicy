KCOS Capture — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8.1.2026

KCOS Capture (“the app”) is an internal application provided by Kingsbury Companies (“Kingsbury,” “we,” “us”) for our employees and approved partners to photograph jobsites and upload those photos to Kingsbury’s project system (KCOS). This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Photos you choose to upload. When you capture or select a photo and tap upload, the app sends that image, its file name, and its capture date/time to KCOS so it can be filed under the correct project.
  • Account identity. You sign in with your Microsoft (Entra ID) account. We receive your name, email/username, and a user identifier to authenticate you and to record who uploaded each photo.
  • Basic operational data. Limited technical information needed to run the app reliably (for example, error and connectivity status for the upload queue).

How we use it

We use this information solely to (1) sign you in, (2) show you the projects you’re authorized to access, and (3) attach the photos you upload to the correct project in KCOS. We do not sell your data, share it with third parties for advertising, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Device permissions

The app requests access to your camera and photo library only to let you take or select the photos you choose to upload. It does not access other content, and it does not upload anything you don’t explicitly select and send.

Storage and retention

Uploaded photos and related records are stored within Kingsbury’s KCOS system and its service providers, and are retained according to Kingsbury’s business record-keeping practices.

Access

Access to the app and to uploaded photos is limited to authorized Kingsbury personnel and approved partners.

Your choices

You control which photos you upload. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact us at the address below.
